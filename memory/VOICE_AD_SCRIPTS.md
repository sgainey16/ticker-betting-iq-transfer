# The Ticker — Voice Ad Scripts (Reggie & Marc)

**Status:** Ready to record. Save for Phase 2 marketing push — right before we open paid conversion.
**Voices:** Reggie Banks (warm, streetwise, leans in) + Marc Collins (dry, analyst, drops the number).
**Target length:** 20–30 seconds each. Runs between broadcast segments in the Show, and on Landing before signup.

---

## 🎙️ AD #1 — Betting IQ + Fantasy Tracker · "We Study You"
*The core positioning line. Punch it hard. This is the one that flips people from "cool AI show" to "wait, this thing knows me."*

> **MARC** *(analyst, dry, matter-of-fact):*
> "Every other tool out there studies the game. Studies the odds. Studies the model."
>
> **REGGIE** *(leaning in, warmer, a beat slower):*
> "We study **you**. Your history. Your patterns. The questions you ask before you bet. The blind spots you can't see yourself."
>
> **MARC:**
> "Betting IQ. The Fantasy Tracker. That's how you actually get better."
>
> **REGGIE** *(sign-off, half-smile):*
> "Coach, not casino. Back after this."

## Slot placements
- **On Show** — plays automatically between segments 2 and 3 (post-Reggie-Marc opener, before deep-dive segment).
- **Landing / Home** — muted auto-play with captions when a first-visit user lingers 15+ seconds without pressing Play.
- **Founding Member paywall** — the moment a user hits a paid feature (e.g., taps "Deep Dive analytics"), this ad plays before the upsell card. Sound-on default.

## Production notes
- Reggie's "we study **you**" — the word "you" should carry the emphasis. Not shouted — leaned into. Almost intimate.
- Marc's "Betting IQ. The Fantasy Tracker." — deliberate pause between the two products, like he's listing serious tools.
- Reggie's sign-off — the "coach, not casino" line is our permanent tagline. Every ad should end with it.
