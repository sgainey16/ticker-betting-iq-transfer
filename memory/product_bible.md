## Core Features

**Live AI Panel** — Four analysts discussing today's sports.

**One-on-One Press Conference** — Premium feature. Walk onto the stage. Sit down with one analyst. Ask unlimited questions. Exactly like interviewing a sports analyst.

**Fantasy Assistant** — Who should I start? Who should I trade? Waiver pickups. Prospects. Goalies. Advanced metrics.

**Betting Assistant** — Expected goals, line movement, player props, matchups, rest days, travel, back-to-backs, historical trends, value opportunities, risk analysis.

**Prediction Center** — Every analyst makes predictions. Users compare. Track records matter. Leaderboards.

**Community** — Users vote. Debate. Challenge analysts. Earn reputation.

**Games** — Trivia, prediction contests, Beat the Analysts, mini games. Win subscriptions, merchandise, trips, VIP experiences.

## Personality Philosophy

Characters matter more than technology.
Technology can be copied.
Personalities cannot.

Every character needs:
Distinct speech, humor, history, flaws, confidence, body language, facial expressions, catchphrases, vocabulary, rhythm, opinions.

No two characters should sound remotely alike.

## Main Cast

**Reggie Banks** — Chicago. Blue collar. Naturally funny. Never trying to be funny. Quick wit. Master chirper. Makes everyone else uncomfortable. Heart of gold. Will defend friends instantly.

**Marc Collins** — Lovable. Balanced. Keeps discussions moving. Professional. Great interviewer. Fans trust him. Acts as moderator.

**Tank** — Former enforcer. Big personality. Old school. Protective. Loyal. Simple. Huge heart.

## Character Development

Each personality eventually receives 1000+ quotes, stories, opinions, speech patterns, favorite sayings, life experiences, running jokes, emotional triggers, friends, rivals, music, food, movies, childhood, career, beliefs. Everything becomes consistent. Characters become real.

## Humor

The humor drives retention. Not slapstick. Not stand-up comedy. Real locker-room banter. Constant chirping. Categories: Bald, Fat, Skinny, Short, Old, Young, Hairline, Cheap, Fashion, Golf game, Skating, Hands, Stick, Tape jobs, Coffee, Dad jokes, Airport stories, Road trips, Equipment, Locker room habits, Hotel stories, Media personalities, Current NHL storylines. Nothing malicious. Just believable locker-room fun.

## Speaking Style

Nobody delivers long speeches. Natural interruptions. Overlap. Someone laughs. Someone disagrees. Someone changes subject. Exactly like real television.

## Visual Direction

Characters should have 20+ expressions each: Talking, Laughing, Rolling eyes, Looking sideways, Checking notes, Reading stats, Looking at another host, Looking at camera, Thinking, Celebrating, Disappointed, Confused, Excited, Serious, Smirking, Pointing, Leaning back, Crossed arms, Writing notes, Watching monitor. Emergent should cycle these naturally. Never static.

## Technology Requirements

- High-quality conversational AI with long-term character memory.
- Consistent personality prompting and retrieval from a centralized "Character Bible."
- Real-time NHL data integration (scores, rosters, injuries, advanced stats, betting lines, fantasy data).
- Speech synthesis with unique voices and emotional variation.
- Animated avatars capable of natural idle behavior, eye contact changes, gestures, and conversational turn-taking.
- Conversation orchestration so panelists interrupt, agree, disagree, joke, and build on each other's comments rather than speaking in isolation.
- User profiles that remember favorite teams, leagues, analysts, preferred tone, and past conversations.
- Prediction engine with persistent accuracy tracking and public leaderboards.
- Scalable architecture that allows new sports, analysts, and features to be added without rebuilding the core system.

## Phase 1 MVP (One-Month Goal)

The objective is not to build the finished vision.
The objective is to prove that people love spending time with the personalities.

The MVP should include:
- Hockey only.
- Two-panel format (Reggie + Marc), since the chemistry between two hosts is the key differentiator.
- Live NHL scores and news.
- AI question-and-answer capability.
- Fantasy hockey assistant.
- Basic betting insights.
- Character memory and consistent personalities.
- Premium "Press Conference" mode for one-on-one conversations.
- A polished studio environment with premium branding.
- Subscription-ready architecture.

If users come back because they miss talking to Reggie and Marc, the foundation is working.

## Success Metric

The ultimate goal isn't downloads. It isn't AI accuracy. It isn't even subscription revenue.

The real measure of success is this: Fans should eventually say, "I wonder what Reggie thinks about that trade," the same way people today wonder what a favorite TV analyst or podcast host thinks.

When the characters become part of hockey culture, The Ticker stops being an app and becomes a media brand.
